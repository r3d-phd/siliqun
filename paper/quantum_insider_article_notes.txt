Quantum Insider Article: "Researchers Scale Quantum Circuit Simulation Beyond 40 Qubits"
Published: April 2, 2026
Source: https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/04/02/quantum-circuit-simulation-beyond-40-qubits/

KEY FACTS:
- Joint team: Osaka University QIQB + Fixstars Corporation
- Used 1,024 NVIDIA H100 GPUs on AIST's ABCI-Q system
- Surpassed previous 40-qubit limit for state-vector quantum circuit simulation
- Focus: quantum chemistry (IQPE circuits)

RESULTS:
- Largest problem size: 42-spin-orbital system for H2O (with qubit reduction)
- Largest circuit size: 41-qubit circuit for Fe2S2 molecule
- Simulator: "chemqulacs-gpu" (GPU-based quantum circuit simulator)
- Key innovation: parallel computing method for large-scale GPU clusters
- Resolved inter-GPU communication bottlenecks

APPROACH:
- State-vector based simulation (full Hilbert space)
- IQPE (Iterative Quantum Phase Estimation) - requires fewer qubits than standard QPE
- Multi-GPU distribution across 1,024 GPUs
- 48-hour computation window

APPLICATION DOMAIN:
- Quantum chemistry for drug discovery and materials science
- Fault-tolerant quantum computing (FTQC) algorithm validation
