# SiliQun V2 B3 Rebaselined Algorithm-Representation Export Authority V2

`AUTHORIZE_SILIQUN_V2_B3_REBASELINED_TWO_QUBIT_FIXED_IDEAL_EXPORT_ONLY`

The original B3 source-export attempt was refused before a receipt was written because the proposed third representation required three qubits while `simos-nominal-literature-v1` declares a two-qubit capacity. This V2 authority applies only after the new two-qubit static specification receipt, readiness receipt, and independently generated SiVQD static specification pins have passed and been published.

This authority permits **one** local source export of exactly the rebaselined three-representation suite: the two-qubit balanced-oracle Deutsch–Jozsa representation, the two-qubit fixed-phase-oracle Grover representation, and the two-qubit uncontrolled restricted work-orbit transition for multiplication by 2 on the encoding `1,2,4,8` modulo 15. It permits translation through `TranslationRequest`, ideal state-vector evaluation, canonical probability-digest creation, digest-only receipt writing, source audit, and local tests.

The rebaselined third representation is not controlled modular multiplication, order finding, a Shor execution, or a factorization computation. This authority does **not** permit a named-device profile, provider or hardware access, pulse export, calibration, physical or noise claim, PPO, SiMORA import/invocation, detector/decoder/frame action, algorithm ingress, algorithm answer, measurement result, success probability, order, factor, QEC, or performance result. Raw state and probability vectors remain transient and may not be retained.
